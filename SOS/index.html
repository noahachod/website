SOS Violence Conjugale

SOS Violence Conjugale is a non-profit organization whose mission is to help ensure the safety of victims of intimate partner violence throughout Quebec.

I animated a text conversation to introduce SOS's services, highlighting their 24/7 availability and their ability to provide direct access to information, support, and shelter.

Optimizing for Accessibility

The animation was created specifically for the SOS Violence Conjugale website. It needed to be delivered as a GIF under a strict file-size limit, with specific dimensions, to ensure it could load reliably for visitors, regardless of their internet connection.

This meant carefully adjusting the colours and compression while preserving the look and feel of the animation, working at 12 fps, and using a bit of Python to help optimize the final file.

It was a really fulfilling challenge. It's always rewarding to create work with accessibility in mind, knowing that it can reach as many people as possible.
